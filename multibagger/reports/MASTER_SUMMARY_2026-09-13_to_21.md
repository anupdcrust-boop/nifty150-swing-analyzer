# Multibagger Coverage — Master Summary (2026-09-13 to 2026-09-21)

36 stocks analyzed over 9 daily cycles, one per market-cap band per day (Nifty 50 / Midcap 100 sample / Smallcap 250 sample / Microcap 250 sample), picked at random without repeats until each band's list cycles through. **Result across all 36: zero BUY or ACCUMULATE.** 33 landed at WATCH, 3 at AVOID.

Every figure behind every verdict is tagged by source tier (Tier 1-5) in the underlying dossier JSON — most are Tier 3 (aggregator-via-search) or Tier 5 (reconstructed), not Tier 1/2 audited-filing-verified. This is a directional screen built and run in an environment without live financial-data access, not investment research. See `multibagger/docs/FRAMEWORK_v2.2_FULL.md` for the methodology and `multibagger/sample_data/daily/*.json` for full per-field sourcing on any name below.

## Overall patterns across all 36

1. **Zero BUY/ACCUMULATE.** The framework's own hardest gate (Section 5e engine growth) was the single most common blocker — but 4 names actually cleared it outright (Sharda Cropchem, Alkem [flagged as a lookback artifact], Bajaj Finserv, REC, BEML) and 2 more landed as genuine near-misses (Balkrishna Industries, Aavas Financiers), validating the graduated near-miss band added in v2.2.
2. **The binding constraint was usually N7 (variant perception), not weak fundamentals.** In the large majority of cases, agents explicitly declined to construct a "market has it wrong" thesis because the evidence didn't support one — that's the framework's M3 edge-hypothesis rule working as intended, not a research shortfall.
3. **Real governance findings surfaced, not manufactured ones:** Aavas's MD/CFO/CRO resignation cluster + open NHB inspection; Ujjivan's rejected universal-bank licence; IOB's overdue shareholding-compliance deadline; Sharda Cropchem's and Alkem's promoter-holding declines.
4. **Corporate-structure catches mattered:** REC-PFC merger, SKF India's Oct-2025 demerger, ITC Hotels' Jan-2025 demerger, Eicher's VECV equity-accounting, Mahamaya Steel's/SKF's ticker-identity disambiguation from similarly-named but unrelated companies.
5. **Two real framework gaps found and one fixed** (documented in `FRAMEWORK_v2.2.md`): the G1 BFSI leverage carve-out (fixed), and the Irani Accounting-Gap/Leverage-Trap BFSI carve-out (found, not yet fixed).
